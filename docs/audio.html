VAU audio

The same units, tuned for an audio model and heard on it: 13 of them, with the names, defaults and ranges that work on Magenta RT 2 small, the presets measured there, and each unit's sweep to listen to. The units on a language model are VAU text.

The Audio Collection is the Core Collection's units tuned for an audio model: the same files and the same edits, with the names, defaults, ranges, host-feature settings and placements that were found to work on Magenta RT 2 small, where a position is a 40 ms frame, counted from the start of play and the first position is no attention sink. A host loads it with vau.Registry(collection="audio"); what it changes is one file, src/vau/collections/audio.json (SPEC 5h). Hosts whose model runs in MLX can use vau.mlx, the MLX versions of Neuron, Quantise and Abliterate.

Each unit's sweep is Magenta RT 2 small playing a warm drone (seed 1), one control moved at a time from the unit's example line, the rest held, rendered once and recorded. Original is the model unchanged. A setting marked ! fell silent or overdrove there. The sweeps start from the Core Collection's example lines, not from this collection's defaults: the settings at which Lookback and Running Average fell silent are why the collection turns Keep size on for both. A preset's own line was not rendered; choosing one marks the nearest setting of each control.

Abliterate

abliteratecomponent

Removes a direction from what attention and MLPs write, with a strength that peaks at one block and tapers

Strength runs to 6 here; the Core Collection stops at 1.5. Up to 1 the direction is taken out, from 1 to 2 it is turned round, and past 2 the sound is pushed further to the far side. Taking a direction out pulls a sound toward the middle of that direction, so a sound far along it changes most: on Deep techno, Pulse at strength 1 took the centroid from 69 Hz to 902 Hz, while on the Warm drone, which sits a little to the dark side of Brightness, Brightness at 1 raised it from 138 to 191 Hz. Almost all of the writing along Brightness and Pulse is done in block 0 and blocks 9 to 11, so on a single block in between the unit changes little; it starts on all blocks, flat, with Keep size off. Its MLX version (vau.mlx) keeps it on the GPU, so on a host that runs in MLX all twelve blocks cost no more than none.

Help

Removes the component along a direction from what each chosen block's attention and MLP write. Strength is the share removed at the peak (1 removes it all; above 1 reverses some of it). Peak is the block where the strength is greatest, as a share of the model's depth (0 the first block, 1 the last); width, also a share of the depth, is how far it reaches either side; edge is the strength at that reach, as a share of the peak. Shape: linear tapers in a straight line (Heretic's kernel), bell along a Gaussian (OBLITERATUS), flat removes the same share at every chosen block. MLP share scales the strength for the MLPs. Projected first makes the direction orthogonal to the stream's mean at that layer (Lai's projected abliteration); Keep size scales each output feature back to its weight row's length (Lai's norm-preserving abliteration); Bias too removes the direction from the bias as well. Each block uses the direction at the layer it writes into. Strength 0 is neutral.

Help

A neuron is one of the MLP's hidden units (3,072 per block in GPT-2 small): its activation, after the nonlinearity, is what the MLP's output projection reads, and the projection's column for it is what the neuron writes into the stream. n names one neuron or several (n=373,2006), counted from 0. The operation acts on each named neuron's activation at every position: scale multiplies it by the value (1 is neutral, 0 zeroes, −1 flips), add adds the value, set replaces it with the value, zero and flip take no value, clamp holds it within ± the value. The finder in the bench's Directions panel lists neurons that fire differently where the model is about to write a word.
